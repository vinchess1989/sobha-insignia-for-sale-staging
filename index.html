Overview

A Signature Sobha Address on the ORR

This is a 3.5 bedroom (3 BHK + study/home office) residence on the 6th floor of Wing 1 at Sobha Insignia, Bhoganahalli Village, Varthur Hobli — on Bengaluru's Sarjapur‑Marathahalli Outer Ring Road corridor, close to Bellandur.

The apartment offers 1,524.72 sft of carpet area and 110.12 sft of balcony space, totalling 2,403.21 sft of Super Built‑up Area inclusive of a proportionate share of common areas — along with two dedicated car parking spaces in the basement/ground floor.

Built by Sobha Limited to their signature specification — vitrified tile flooring, timber doors, premium chromium‑plated bath fittings, and full video door phone / intercom wiring — the project is a self‑contained development of 2 basements + ground + 8 floors, with a rooftop clubhouse and swimming pool.

Construction is complete, with handover targeted for August 2026. This is a direct, no‑broker resale from the original owner.
