## 2. Tech Stack & Architecture
- **Frontend:** Pure semantic HTML5, modern Vanilla CSS3, responsive mobile-first typography and photo grid.
- **Assets:** Optimized floorplans, architectural renderings, site views, and common area amenity photo galleries in `assets/`.
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages with `.nojekyll` bypass.
- **SEO & OpenGraph:** Fully configured meta tags (`og:title`, `og:image`, `og:description`, `twitter:card`) ensuring high-fidelity previews when shared via WhatsApp, LinkedIn, and Telegram.

---

## 3. Major Features
1. **Interactive Apartment Showcase:** Comprehensive display of 3.5 BHK floorplans, room dimensions, super built-up area (2,403 sft), and carpet area specs.
2. **Direct WhatsApp & Phone Contact Actions:** Integrated one-click WhatsApp chat triggers with pre-populated inquiry text and direct phone links.
3. **High-Resolution Photo Gallery:** Responsive lightbox gallery highlighting clubhouse, swimming pool, terrace deck, and green landscape amenities.
4. **Transparent Cost Sheet Breakdown:** Transparent pricing breakdown (₹4.5 Cr all-inclusive) without broker commission overhead.
5. **Fast Mobile Performance:** Zero heavy JS frameworks, loading under 1 second with optimized image delivery.
